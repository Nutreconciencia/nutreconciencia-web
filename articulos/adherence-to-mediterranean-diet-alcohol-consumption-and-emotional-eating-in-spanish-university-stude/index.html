Adherence to Mediterranean Diet, Alcohol Consumption and Emotional Eating in Spanish University Students

Miguel López-Moreno, Marta Garcés-Rimón, Marta Miguel, María Teresa Iglesias López

Resumen científico

Lo esencial en menos de un minuto.

A total of 584 university students aged 20.5 (sex ratio = 0.39) were enrolled in a cross-sectional study. In total, 63.6% of students showed low adherence to the Mediterranean diet, with no differences by sex (64.3% female versus 61.5% male, p = 0.19). According to the AUDIT questionnaire, 26.2% of participants were categorized as high-risk drinkers and 7.7% as ADS. About 38.6% of the students were categorized as eating very emotionally or eating emotionally, and 37.2% were categorized as low emotional eaters. A weak positive correlation was observed between the EEQ and BMI in female students (rho= 0.15, p = 0.03). (4)
