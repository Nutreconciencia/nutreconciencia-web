Longitudinal changes in motivational determinants of sustainable diets during a pilot blended digital behavior change intervention: A mixed methods study.

Laura Bervian, Ujué Frésán, Joren Buekers, Miguel López-Moreno, Sergi Fàbregues, Claudia Teran-Escobar, Guillaume Chevance

Resumen científico

Lo esencial en menos de un minuto.

Group-level quantitative analyses demonstrated small (0.02-0.04 points/week on a 0-10 scale) but significant increases in environmental and ethical motivations, habit strength, and adherence to a more sustainable diet, alongside a decrease in price as motivation for food choice. Individual-level results showed important heterogeneity of both motives and behaviors, and within-person network analysis showed no covariation between motivational determinant change and diet change.

Qué hicieron

Diseño del estudio

The adoption of sustainable diets in high-income countries is an urgent environmental and health priority. This mixed methods study explored longitudinal motivational determinants change during a year-long blended digital intervention promoting sustainable diet change in healthy omnivorous adults. The 22-week intervention comprised app-delivered motivational and informational text messages (focusing on self-monitoring, information about health and environmental consequences and instructions on how to perform a behavior), and tailored online feedback sessions.

QUÉ ENCONTRARON

Principales resultados

Group-level quantitative analyses demonstrated small (0.02-0.04 points/week on a 0-10 scale) but significant increases in environmental and ethical motivations, habit strength, and adherence to a more sustainable diet, alongside a decrease in price as motivation for food choice. Individual-level results showed important heterogeneity of both motives and behaviors, and within-person network analysis showed no covariation between motivational determinant change and diet change.

INTERPRETACIÓN

Cómo interpretarlo

This study demonstrates the potential for blended digital interventions to influence motivational pathways related to sustainable diets and supports using mixed methods designs to track the dynamic dietary behavior change process.
